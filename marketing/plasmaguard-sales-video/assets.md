## What to ask PlasmaGuard for

Ask for all of it in one request, alongside the written permission.

1. **Sensor LCD footage** — twenty seconds of a real reading changing. This is
   the single highest-value asset in the whole production. Scene 4 is built
   around it and currently falls back to a still.
2. **Installation footage** — a generator going onto a duct wall. Scene 7.
3. **Their existing videos.** plasmaguard.com/videos/ publishes four:
   *PlasmaGuard PRO for Commercial Markets*, *…for the Maritime Industry*,
   *…for Mass Transportation*, and *Tested by the EPA*. Ask for the source files
   rather than ripping the embeds.
4. **Brand asset pack** — logo in vector, correct colours, correct trademark
   usage. What we have are 1080px squares from a WordPress media library.
5. **Case studies.** Their site lists a Case Studies section. A named building
   with a real outcome would be worth more than any other asset on this list,
   and it is the one thing this video cannot currently offer.
6. **The datasheet PDFs** from plasmaguard.com/literature/ — useful as a
   follow-up attachment after the sizing conversation in scene 12.

## Claim rules attached to the assets

- **Product photography** may be scaled. It may not be cropped to remove a
  feature, recoloured, composited into a scene that implies an installation we
  cannot show, or have text overlaid that makes a claim.
- **Their video footage**, if supplied, carries their claims. It may be trimmed
  but not re-cut in a way that changes what a claim applies to — particularly
  anything from *Tested by the EPA*, which is a test, not an endorsement.
- **Generic b-roll** (lobbies, offices, HVAC plant rooms) must be licensed stock
  or your own footage. It may not be framed to look like a PlasmaGuard
  installation.
- **No AI-generated footage of the product.** See rule 1 in the README.

## Music

Licensed library track only, low and unobtrusive, ducked under the voiceover
throughout and out entirely under the end card. No dramatic sting on the 99.99%
slide — a claim that needs a drum hit reads as a claim that needs help.
