Shipping & Delivery

The Training Program is delivered online. Physical partner products are sold, charged and shipped by the companies that make them.

The Training Program: digital delivery

  • The {{ program.name }} is a digital service. Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.
  • Access starts as soon as your account is created. You sign in with the email address and password you chose.
  • You use {{ course.name }} and the back office online at {{ site.app_url }}, in any modern web browser.
  • If you can't sign in or a lesson won't play, let us know through our contact form.

Partner products: sold and shipped by the partner company

When you order a partner product through a partner's product page, you buy it directly from the partner company, on its own checkout. The partner company charges your card, ships your order, and handles returns and warranty. {{ business.legal_name }} does not take payment for, stock or ship these products.
