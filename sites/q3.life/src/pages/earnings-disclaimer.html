There is no guarantee that you will earn any money. You may earn little or nothing, and you may spend more on your membership and expenses than you earn in commissions.

No promise of income

{{ business.legal_name }} does not promise or guarantee that you will earn any commission, cover the cost of your membership, or replace any part of your income. Your membership fee pays for access to the course, the back office and support, not for a chance to earn.

What your results depend on

Anything you earn depends on factors we don't control, including your own effort, skills, time, network, location and the economy, and on the choices other people make. Past results, including anyone else's, do not predict yours.

Typical earnings

The Partner Program is new, so we don't yet have enough results to publish figures that would be representative. Until we do, we make no statement about typical or average earnings. Anyone who quotes you an earnings figure for {{ site.brand }} is not speaking for us.

Costs to consider

  • The membership fee: {{ membership.price_display }} {{ membership.interval_phrase }} once your billing starts, charged every {{ membership.interval_noun }} until you cancel.

What {{ site.brand }} is not

  • Membership is not an investment, and nothing you pay buys equity, profit share or a return.
  • Partnership is not a job. Partners are independent contractors, not employees.
  • Nothing on this website or in the course is financial, legal or tax advice.

Statements by partners

Partners are not allowed to make income claims (see Rules for partners). If a partner has told you that you will earn a particular amount, please tell us. It breaks our rules, and we act on every report.
