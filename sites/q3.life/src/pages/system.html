PlasmaGuard PRO™ Generator

Continuously cleans indoor air and surfaces

The PlasmaGuard PRO Generator integrates directly into the HVAC system. Its proprietary non-thermal, cold plasma energy core charges and divides air molecules, generating a patented blend of long-lasting positive and negative nitrogen and oxygen ions. These ions travel through the air ducts and into the occupied spaces, making every vent in the building an air purification unit. Our technology works at the source of the problem, removing airborne pathogens and particulates while cleansing surfaces.

  • Neutralizes odors, smoke, and mold spores*

  • Reduced particulate levels are proven with the Sensor’s laser particle counter

PlasmaGuard PRO™ Sensor

Actively monitors and responds to changes in indoor air quality

In-room sensors actively detect and alert you to the levels of particles in the indoor air. The sensors use a laser particle counter to monitor the indoor environment as air particles pass through it. When the sensor detects changes in indoor air quality, it activates a response to manage airborne aerosols and particles while demonstrating its performance.

  • Proprietary patented response technology delivers and maintains optimal air quality
  • Continuously measures and monitors indoor air and responds to changes in real-time
  • Measures particulates down to 0.3 microns in size, including PM1, PM2.5, and PM10
  • Reduced particulate levels are proven on the LCD indicator
  • Plugs directly into a standard wall outlet or can be used with a standard USB cable
  • 2-year limited warranty

PlasmaGuard PRO™ Smart Hub

Real-time air quality management

The Smart Hub enables the PlasmaGuard PRO System to manage and maintain the indoor air quality in real-time. The automated control system integrates the Smart Hub with the Sensors and Generator. When indoor air quality changes are detected, the Sensor notifies the Smart Hub, and the Generator responds. The Smart Hub is compatible with Zigbee-enabled protocols and cloud-connected automation devices.
