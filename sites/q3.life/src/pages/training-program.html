Questions

Training Program FAQ

Do I have to buy this to be a partner?

No. A partner account is free and takes no card. You can refer the {{ vendor.product }} and be paid commission on confirmed sales without ever subscribing to {{ course.name }}. There are no sales quotas and no required purchases.

How do I get an invitation?

Ask the person who told you about {{ site.brand }} for their referral link. If you don't know a partner, send us a request through our contact form. The account itself is free; the Training Program is added later from Billing if you want it.

Can I cancel before my first charge?

Yes. Cancel from Billing any time before your first charge and you will not be charged. Billing always shows when your first charge will happen. Until the program opens there is nothing to cancel, because nothing has been started.

What happens to my access when I cancel?

You keep access to {{ course.name }} until the end of the {{ program.interval_noun }} you paid for, and you are not charged again. After that the course access ends. Your partner account is not affected — it is free, and your referral link, product pages and commissions carry on.

Is my payment information safe?

Card details are entered into Stripe's secure payment form and sent directly to Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. They never pass through or get stored on our servers. Every page of our site and member platform is served over HTTPS.

Who can join?

Adults aged {{ policies.min_age }} or older who live in {{ business.service_region }}, with a free partner account. See Eligibility in our Terms.
