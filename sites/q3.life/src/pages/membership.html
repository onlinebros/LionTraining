Questions

Membership FAQ

Do I have to refer anyone to be a member?

No. {{ course.name }}, the back office and support are yours whether or not you ever share a referral link. There are no sales quotas and no required purchases.

How do I get an invitation?

Ask the person who told you about {{ site.brand }} for their referral link. If you don't know a partner, send us a request through our contact form.

Can I cancel before my first charge?

Yes. Cancel from Billing any time before your first charge and you will not be charged. Billing always shows when your first charge will happen.

What happens to my access when I cancel?

You keep access until the end of the {{ membership.interval_noun }} you paid for, and you are not charged again. After that, your course access and partner tools end.

Is my payment information safe?

Card details are entered into Stripe's secure payment form and sent directly to Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. They never pass through or get stored on our servers. Every page of our site and member platform is served over HTTPS.

Who can join?

Adults aged {{ policies.min_age }} or older who live in {{ business.service_region }}. See Eligibility in our Terms.
