Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancel any time before your first charge and you pay nothing. Once your card has been charged, Training Program fees are not refundable.

Last updated {{ policies.updated_display }}

Training Program charges are non-refundable. If you don't want to be charged, cancel before your first charge, or before your next billing date. Billing always shows when your first charge will happen and your next billing date.

How to cancel

  • Online (fastest): sign in, open Billing and choose to cancel the Training Program. It takes effect straight away, and the Billing page shows the date your access ends.
  • Through our contact form: send a request from our contact form, choosing "Billing or cancellation" and using the email address on your account.

To avoid any charge, cancel before your first charge. After a charge, cancelling stops the next one. If you change your mind before your access ends, you can resume from the same Billing page.

Duplicate or mistaken charges

If you were charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or charged in error, send a request through our contact form and choose "Billing or cancellation". Include the email address on your account and the date and amount of the charge. We refund confirmed mistakes in full to the card that was charged. Your bank usually takes {{ policies.refund_processing }} to show the refund.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, we email you and retry the card. Your access continues for {{ policies.payment_grace_days }} days while you update your card in Billing. If payment still hasn't gone through after that, access pauses until it does or until you cancel.

Before you dispute a charge

Training Program charges appear on card statements as {{ program.statement_descriptor }}. If you don't recognize a charge, or think it is wrong, please contact us through our contact form before disputing it with your bank.

Partner products

Partner products are sold by our partner companies, not by {{ business.legal_name }}. The partner company takes the payment on its own checkout, and it handles refunds, returns, exchanges and warranty claims under its own policies. We cannot issue refunds for partner products.
