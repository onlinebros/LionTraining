1. How the program works

Partners share {{ site.brand }} and our partner products with people they know, using their personal referral link and product pages. Partners can earn commissions on qualifying sales, as defined in the Compensation Plan.

  • Commissions are only ever paid on completed, paid sales, and are reversed if a sale is refunded or charged back.
  • There are no sales quotas, no volume requirements, and no products you have to buy or hold as inventory.
  • Your back office shows your referral link, your partner organization, and a statement of any commissions.

2. Your partner organization

When someone joins with your referral link, they are placed directly beneath you in your partner organization, and the people they refer are placed beneath them. You can see your organization in your back office.

Placement always follows referral. We do not move people between organizations to fill positions. The Compensation Plan will explain how sales made within your organization are treated.

3. Partner products

Customers can buy products from manufacturers we work with through a partner's product page. The manufacturer is the seller and charges the customer directly, and {{ site.brand }} receives a fee on each completed sale. Our first partner company is {{ vendor.legal_name }}. Commissions on partner-product sales are set out in the Compensation Plan.

4. The Compensation Plan

The Compensation Plan will set out:

  • which sales qualify for commission, and the commission rates
  • how sales made within your partner organization are treated
  • who is eligible to earn, including what happens if a payment fails or a membership ends
  • how long commissions are held before they can be paid, and how refunds and chargebacks are handled
  • when payouts are made, and any minimum balance

Every member agrees to the Compensation Plan in force at the time. We give at least 30 days' email notice before the plan takes effect or changes. Commissions on earlier sales are paid under the plan that applied when the sale was made.

5. Payouts and taxes

Commissions are paid by bank transfer through Stripe. To receive payouts you must complete Stripe's identity verification and give the tax information US law requires.

Partners are independent contractors, not employees, agents or franchisees of {{ business.legal_name }}. You are responsible for your own taxes and expenses. Where the law requires, we report commission payments to the IRS.

6. Rules for partners

These rules protect customers. Breaking them can cost you commissions or your membership (see Terms of Service). As a partner you must:

  • Make no income claims. Never promise or suggest that anyone will earn a particular amount, cover their membership cost, or replace their income. Never present your own earnings as typical.
  • Make no product claims beyond the manufacturer's. Describe partner products only in the manufacturer's own words, and make no health or medical claims.
  • Disclose the relationship. Whenever you share a referral link or recommend a product, say clearly that you may earn a commission.
  • Describe {{ site.brand }} accurately. Say what membership costs, that it renews every {{ membership.interval_noun }}, and that earnings are not guaranteed. Never present membership as an investment or a job.
  • Contact people lawfully. No spam, no unsolicited automated calls or texts, and respect every opt-out.
  • Do not pay other people's fees or offer them inducements to join, and do not open more than one membership.
  • Do not use our name in ways we have not approved, including in ads, domain names or social media handles.

7. Questions

Do I have to recruit anyone or sell anything?

No. You can use {{ course.name }} and the back office and never share a link. Your membership costs the same either way.

Is there a separate fee to be a partner?

No. The Partner Program is included in the {{ membership.name }} ({{ membership.price_display }} {{ membership.interval_phrase }} once your billing starts). There is no extra charge to take part.

When will the Compensation Plan be published?

Before it takes effect. We will post it on this page and email every member at least 30 days in advance.

How much do partners earn?

We can't tell you. The program is new, the Compensation Plan is still being finalized, and we won't quote figures until we have real results to report. You may earn little or nothing. Please read the Earnings Disclaimer before you decide.
